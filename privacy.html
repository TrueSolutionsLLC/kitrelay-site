Privacy policy · October22,2026

Your equipment stays yours.

KitRelay’s core inventory is stored on your iPhone. There is no developer-operated inventory service or app account.

App data

Kits, equipment details, outing snapshots, packing and return counts, and follow-ups are stored in the app’s local container. The app does not send this inventory to the developer. It includes no advertising, analytics, tracking SDK or remote AI service. It has no automatic cross-device sync.

Apple purchases and device backups

Apple processes trial and lifetime confirmations. KitRelay reads verified Apple transactions to determine access. The developer does not receive your payment details. Apple may process purchases and device backups according to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies.

Exports you choose

JSON backups and CSV exports contain details you entered. You choose where to save or share them. Destination services handle those files under their own policies. Restoring a backup preserves a copy of the previous local file before replacement.

Support email

If you contact support, the developer receives your address, message and any information you choose to attach. This is used to answer and resolve the support request. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information. Contact computerhelperstl@gmail.com for privacy questions or to request removal of support correspondence where applicable.

Website hosting

This static website contains no analytics, advertising, forms or inventory upload. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process standard connection information, including IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Deletion

Removing the app removes its local inventory. Backups and exports saved elsewhere are separate and remain under your control. Apple-managed purchases and device backups are governed by Apple’s settings and policies.

Changes

This policy will be updated if data practices change. The date above identifies this version.

Equipment, accounted for

There.
And back.

Reusable equipment lists for the people who pack cameras, cables, instruments and field gear—then need to bring it all home.

One kit. A fresh record for every outing.

Build a kit once, count what actually leaves, and check it back in. Each outing preserves its own equipment snapshot. Missing pieces and repair notes become follow-ups.

KitRelay is in development and has not been released on the App Store.

Pack with a purpose

Group equipment by case or compartment. Track quantities of identical pieces. Departing locks the packing list; only the pieces you packed are expected back.

Close the loop

Count equipment back in, flag repairs, and confirm any missing pieces before closing. Keep the original return record while resolving follow-ups later.

Your lists, on your iPhone

No app account, ads, analytics or developer-operated server. Export a complete JSON backup or read your lists and history in CSV. There is no automatic device sync.

Planned pricing

Free download, an explicitly confirmed free 7-day Trial, and a separate optional US$1.99 lifetime unlock. Apple shows the localized price. No subscription or automatic trial charge. After the trial, viewing, search, export and backup restoration stay available; creating and editing require lifetime access.
